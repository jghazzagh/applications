R-T-C-F: Basic text prompt

Fast text prompts for daily writing, summarizing, revising, and brainstorming.

Role: Act as a friendly instructional designer.
Task: Rewrite this announcement so it is clearer for online students.
Context: Students are in Week 1 and may be new to Brightspace.
Format: Use a short heading, two brief paragraphs, and a 3-item checklist.
CO-STAR+: Advanced text prompt

More control over audience, tone, style, structure, and constraints. Best for polished, public-facing writing.

Context: Our college is launching a faculty workshop on AI literacy.
Objective: Write a promotional email that encourages faculty to register.
Style: Clear, practical, and human.
Tone: Encouraging and not salesy.
Audience: Faculty who are curious about AI.
Response: Subject line, 180-word email, and 3 bullets. Avoid jargon.
S-S-S + Size: Basic image prompt

Simple image prompts that name subject, setting, style, and aspect ratio. Good for quick visuals.

A cozy independent coffee shop interior with a steaming latte on a wooden table,
early morning light through large windows, warm photorealistic photo, 16:9 landscape.
SSCL-CM: Advanced image prompt

Detailed image prompts with camera, lighting, color, and mood control. Best for higher-quality visuals.

A community college instructor helping students use laptops, modern classroom,
wide shot at eye level, soft natural window light, bright blue and orange accents,
clean editorial illustration style, optimistic professional mood, 16:9 landscape,
avoid clutter and distorted hands.

Which framework should I use?

R-T-C-F: Everyday text tasks like emails, announcements, and quick rewrites.

CO-STAR+: Polished, public-facing text where tone and audience matter.

S-S-S + Size: Quick image prompts when you just need a visual fast.

SSCL-CM: Detailed, higher-quality visuals where lighting, color, and composition matter.

Platform tips

ChatGPT: Great for natural back-and-forth refinement.

Claude: Responds well to clearly labeled sections.

Gemini: Good for concise prompts and Google Workspace contexts.

Ideogram: Strong for image text and typography.

Before you publish or submit AI-assisted work

Verify factual claims before using AI-generated text or images.

Revise the wording so the final version sounds like you, not like a generic AI response.

Check that the tone, audience, and purpose still match your original goal.

For images, inspect text, hands, faces, and logos closely before using them.
