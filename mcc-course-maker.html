How to Use MCC Course Maker

Course Maker helps MCC faculty and staff build clean, accessible, brand-aligned content pages for D2L Brightspace with no coding required. Build your page here, copy the HTML, and paste it into your course.

1. Start from a template or a blank page

  1. Open the Page Templates tab to browse seven pre-assembled pages: Blank Page, Content Page, Course AI Policy, Meet Your Instructor, Module Overview, Course Technology, and Textbooks and Materials.
  2. Click Load in Builder to open a template in the Page Builder and customize it, or Copy HTML to grab the finished page as is.

2. Build your page

  1. Every page starts with a banner and a Heading 1. Click the banner or heading text to replace it with your own.
  2. In the Building Blocks panel, click any block to add it to the bottom of your page.
  3. Click and drag a block in the preview to reorder it, or use the up and down arrow buttons that appear when you hover over it.
  4. Click the trash button on a block to remove it.
  5. Click any text in the preview to edit it in place.

3. Add your page to Brightspace

  1. Click Copy Page HTML. The full page code is copied to your clipboard.
  2. In your Brightspace course, go to Content, choose the module you want, then select New > Create a File.
  3. In the editor toolbar, open the Source Code view (the </> icon under Other Insert Options).
  4. Paste the copied HTML, save the source view, give the page a title, and click Save and Close.
Why heading structure matters: headings are how screen reader users navigate a page. Keep one Heading 1 per page and step down in order (H2, then H3) without skipping levels, and never use a heading just to make text big or bold. Every block in this tool is built to WCAG 2.1 contrast requirements: normal text at 4.5:1 or better, large text at 3:1 or better.

4. A few MCC-specific notes

  • All colors come from the MCC brand palette. Orange is used only for accents and large text because MCC orange on white does not meet the 4.5:1 contrast ratio required for body text.
  • Replace image placeholders with your own images and always write meaningful alt text.
  • The generated HTML uses inline styles, so it works in Brightspace without any custom stylesheet.
